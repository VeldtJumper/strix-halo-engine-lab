# Measured gains: the changelog of shipped defaults

[English](measured-gains.md) · [Español](measured-gains.es.md) · [Main README](../README.md)

Every optimization that ships on by default is listed here with what it does, its measured gain, its exactness
evidence and its off switch. The fast path is the default for every engine; `auto` applies the documented admission
guards, and exactness is relative to the named numerical identity. Gains are workload-specific and must not be added.
The per-build campaign tables (for example DeepSeek `69c6bdab` → `6a5604bf`, the Qwen exact-prefill sweep, the GLM
prefill generations) stay on the model pages; the full item lists with preconditions, notices and evidence are in the
[optimization catalog](optimizations.md) ([machine-readable catalog](optimizations.json)); the master
baselines and inspection commands are in [methodology](methodology.md#defaults-and-off-switches).
